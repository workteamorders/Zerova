Permissionless launchpad on Robinhood Chain

Start at 0.Write it in ink.

Every launch mints one token and one Enso: the coin's symbol, brushed on-chain in 12 strokes. The strokes follow the coin's market price upward from its launch price, and come off again if it falls.

How it works

Connect a wallet first. Then launch in six short steps.

How a launch works

Three steps in the Studio, one transaction, no account.

What the code sets, and what it does not promise

Locked liquidity stops the creator from pulling the pool. It does not stop the price from falling.
