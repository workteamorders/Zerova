Permissionless launchpad on Robinhood Chain

Every coin starts at zero. The circle closes.

Each launch mints one token and one Enso, an ink circle painted entirely on-chain. Strokes are added as the coin's market price moves up from its launch price, and removed if it falls back.

Only the completion seal is permanent. Strokes follow the market live, so a painting can lose strokes.

What this transaction does

Launch fee
Exactly 0.0005 ETH. It goes to the factory and can only be withdrawn to the treasury address.
Supply
1,000,000,000 tokens are minted and all of them go into the pool. You receive none.
Liquidity
The pool position is locked in the factory permanently. No function can withdraw it.
Pool fees
You receive 70% of the pool fees that get collected, in amounts nobody can promise. The treasury receives 30%.
Fixed forever
Name, symbol and painting cannot be changed after launch.
Wallet limit
For the first 10 minutes, one wallet can hold at most 2% of supply. This applies to you too. Other people can use many wallets.
No first buy
This transaction does not buy any tokens for you.
Names
Anyone can launch any name. ZEROVA does not screen launches. A name that imitates a brand or another coin can mislead people.
You pay
0.0005 ETH plus network gas. Your wallet shows gas after the transaction is simulated.
