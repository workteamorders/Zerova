What the contract guarantees, and what it does not

Locked liquidity stops the creator from pulling the pool. It does not stop the price from falling.

      Zero Standard

      Five checks, computed from the chain each time a coin's page is read. Each one says literally what it tests. There is no score and no badge.

      They describe how a coin is built. They do not rate its value or its security.

        What this transaction does

        Read the bill, then sign. Some lines are zeros on purpose.

        Launch fee
        Exactly 0.0005 ETH, paid to the factory and withdrawable only to the treasury address.
        Supply
        1,000,000,000 tokens are minted and all of them go into the pool.
        0 to you
        Pool position
        Locked in the factory permanently.
        0 ways out
        First buy
        This transaction buys no tokens for you.
        0
        Changes later
        Name, symbol and painting cannot be changed after launch.
        0
        Pool fees
        You receive 70% of collected pool fees, in amounts nobody can promise. The treasury receives 30%.
        Wallet limit
        First 10 minutes: at most 2% of supply per wallet, you included. Many wallets can get around it.
        Names
        Anyone can launch any name. ZEROVA does not screen launches. A name that imitates a brand or another coin can mislead people.
        You pay
        0.0005 ETH plus gas. Your wallet shows gas after the transaction is simulated.
